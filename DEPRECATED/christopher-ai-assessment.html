DOC-001 Executive Summary

CHRISTOPHER.AI is a distributed systems assessment platform that evaluates architectural stability using state machine-driven workflows. The system processes system specifications through a multi-stage pipeline: signal extraction, evidence retrieval, topology analysis, stability scoring, and report generation. Built with XState v5 for orchestration, it implements fault-tolerant patterns through error handling, degraded states, and deterministic recovery mechanisms. The platform achieves an enterprise-grade fault tolerance rating of 95.75% with clear pathways toward world-class standards through targeted improvements in retry logic, circuit breaking, and chaos engineering integration.
